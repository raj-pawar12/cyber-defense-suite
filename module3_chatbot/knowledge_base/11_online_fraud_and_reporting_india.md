# Online fraud and reporting in India

## Common frauds
Fake KYC update messages, fake customer-care numbers found through search results, UPI collect-request scams, lottery and gift scams, fake job offers asking for fees, loan app harassment, investment and crypto schemes, and digital arrest calls where criminals pretend to be police or customs.

## What to do quickly
Act fast, because speed helps recover money. Call your bank or wallet provider immediately to block cards and accounts. Call the national cybercrime helpline 1930 and file a complaint on the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal at cybercrime.gov.in. Keep screenshots, phone numbers, transaction IDs and messages as evidence. Security incidents can also be reported to CERT-In.

## Remember
Real police, banks and government offices do not ask for OTPs, PINs or money transfers over a phone call. Verify helpline numbers and websites on official sources, because details can change.
