# Common network attacks

## Overview
Denial of service floods a service so real users cannot reach it, and a distributed version (DDoS) uses many devices. A man-in-the-middle attack secretly sits between two parties. ARP and DNS spoofing give false address information to redirect traffic. Brute force guesses passwords repeatedly, and credential stuffing tries leaked passwords on other sites. Port scanning looks for open services. Sniffing reads unencrypted traffic.

## Defences
Use encryption such as TLS so sniffing and tampering are harder. Rate-limit logins and use MFA. Keep services patched and close unused ports. Use firewalls and intrusion detection. Use a content delivery network or DDoS protection for public sites. Monitor logs for unusual spikes.

## Ethics
Testing these attacks is legal only on systems you own or have written permission to test. Use lab environments for practice.
