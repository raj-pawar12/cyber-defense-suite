# Incident response

## The phases
Preparation: policies, backups, contacts and training. Detection: noticing something is wrong through alerts, logs or reports. Containment: stopping the spread, for example by isolating a device. Eradication: removing the cause, such as malware or a stolen account. Recovery: restoring systems and monitoring them. Lessons learned: writing down what happened and improving.

## If your personal account is hacked
Try to log in and change the password, using the official recovery page. Sign out other sessions. Turn on MFA. Check forwarding rules, recovery email and phone number for changes. Warn your contacts not to trust messages from you. Check connected accounts that used the same password.

## Evidence
Keep logs, screenshots and timestamps. Do not delete anything until you know what happened. In an organisation, tell the security team early rather than trying to hide a mistake.
