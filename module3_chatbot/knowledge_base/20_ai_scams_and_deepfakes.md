# AI-powered scams and deepfakes

## What is changing
Criminals use AI to write convincing phishing messages without spelling mistakes, to clone a voice from a short clip, and to make fake video calls. A call from a relative asking for urgent money may be a cloned voice.

## How to protect yourself
Do not trust a voice or face alone. Agree a family code word for emergencies. Hang up and call back on a number you already have. Be suspicious of any request that mixes urgency, secrecy and money. Check the source of shocking videos before sharing.

## For organisations
Require a second approval for payments and bank detail changes. Train staff with realistic examples. Verify unusual executive requests through a separate channel.
