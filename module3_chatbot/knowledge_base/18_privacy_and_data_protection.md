# Privacy and data protection

## Personal habits
Share less. Check privacy settings on social media. Do not post travel plans, ID cards, boarding passes or tickets. Use different emails for different purposes. Review which apps have access to location, camera, microphone and contacts.

## Principles for builders
Collect only the data you need (data minimisation). Say why you collect it. Delete it when it is no longer needed. Protect it with encryption and access control. Let people see and remove their data.

## Law in India
India has the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, which sets duties for organisations that handle personal data and rights for individuals. Rules and dates for its provisions are phased in, so check official sources for the current position.
