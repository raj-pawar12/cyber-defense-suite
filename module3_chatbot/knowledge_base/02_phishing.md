# Phishing

## What phishing is
Phishing is a trick that makes you reveal passwords, OTPs, card details or install malware by pretending to be a trusted sender such as a bank, delivery company, employer or friend. It works by creating urgency, fear or excitement so you act before thinking.

## Types
Email phishing is sent to many people. Spear phishing targets one person using personal details. Whaling targets senior executives. Smishing arrives by SMS or messaging apps. Vishing is a phone call, often pretending to be a bank or police. Quishing hides a malicious link inside a QR code. Clone phishing copies a real email and swaps the link.

## Warning signs
Urgent threats like "your account will be blocked today". Requests for OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords. Generic greetings such as "Dear customer". A sender address or link domain that does not match the real company. Misspelled domains such as paypa1 or amaz0n. Unexpected attachments. Links that hide behind shorteners.

## If you clicked or entered details
Do not panic. Disconnect from the internet if you downloaded a file. Change the password for that account and any other account that used the same password. Turn on multi-factor authentication. Tell your bank immediately if card or UPI details were shared. Report the message to the real company and to the cybercrime authorities.
