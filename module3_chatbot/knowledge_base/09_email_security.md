# Email security

## Why email is targeted
Email is how most phishing, malware and fraud reaches people. Sender names are easy to fake, so the visible name is not proof of identity.

## Authentication records
SPF lists which servers may send mail for a domain. DKIM adds a digital signature that proves the message was not altered. DMARC tells receivers what to do when SPF or DKIM checks fail and sends reports to the domain owner. Together they make spoofing harder, but they do not stop look-alike domains.

## Personal habits
Hover over links to see the real destination. Do not open unexpected attachments, especially archives, scripts and documents asking you to enable macros. Be careful with payment-change requests, a pattern called business email compromise. When in doubt, contact the sender using a phone number from an official source.
