# Mobile security

## Basics
Keep the phone and apps updated. Use a screen lock with a PIN or biometrics. Install apps only from official stores and check the developer and reviews. Review permissions: a torch app does not need your contacts. Turn on remote find and wipe features.

## Common threats
Malicious apps, fake banking apps, SIM-swap fraud where criminals take over your number, SMS phishing, and scams that persuade you to install a screen-sharing app so they can see your OTPs. Never install remote-control apps at the request of a caller.

## Payments
Check the name shown before you approve a UPI payment. You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Do not scan QR codes to receive a payment, because scanning is for paying.
