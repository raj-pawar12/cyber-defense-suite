# Backups and recovery

## The 3-2-1 rule
Keep three copies of important data, on two different kinds of storage, with one copy offline or in a different place. Offline copies survive ransomware that encrypts everything it can reach.

## Good practice
Automate backups so they do not depend on memory. Encrypt backups that leave your control. Test restoring regularly, because a backup you cannot restore is not a backup. Keep several versions so you can go back before a problem began.

## Planning
Decide how much data you can afford to lose (recovery point) and how long you can be down (recovery time). Write the restore steps down before an emergency.
