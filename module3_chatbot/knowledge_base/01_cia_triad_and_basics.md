# Cybersecurity basics: the CIA triad

## The three goals
Confidentiality means only the right people can read data. Integrity means data is not changed without permission. Availability means systems and data are there when needed. Almost every security control protects at least one of these three.

## Threat, vulnerability and risk
A threat is something that can cause harm, such as an attacker or malware. A vulnerability is a weakness, such as unpatched software or a weak password. Risk is the chance that a threat uses a vulnerability, multiplied by the damage it would cause.

## Core principles
Defence in depth uses many layers, so one failure does not mean total failure. Least privilege gives people and programs only the access they need. Fail safe means that when something breaks, it should deny access rather than allow it. Keep security simple, because complex systems hide mistakes.
