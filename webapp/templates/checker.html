    How to spot a fake link yourself

    Read the real domainThe site is the part just before the first single slash. "paypal.com.verify-now.xyz" belongs to verify-now.xyz.
    Watch for look-alikesSwapped letters and numbers, like paypa1 or amaz0n, are a classic trick.
    Ignore the padlockHTTPS only means the connection is encrypted. Scammers use it too.
    Go there directlyIf a message says your account is in trouble, type the official address yourself.
